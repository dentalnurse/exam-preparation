Question types

About the question types

Multiple choice: one question with a list of options. Choose the one best answer.

Extended multiple choice: a theme with a longer list of options, followed by several questions. Choose the best option for each question. Each option may be used once, more than once, or not at all.

NEBDN's specification describes the Knowledge Test as 65 multiple choice questions in 90 minutes, taken online. Extended questions are included here because they are good practice for applying what you know. Always check NEBDN's latest guidance.
