Practice examiner TRIAL

Read a scenario, then talk to the examiner. It listens to your answer and asks follow-up questions about anything you haven't mentioned.

Before you start

This is a practice tool, not an assessment. The examiner checks your answer for key words only. It doesn't understand meaning, so it may ask about something you have already said in different words. It does not mark your work. If anything doesn't seem right, or you want to query something, ask your DNT assessor.
  1. Read the scenario. You have 2.5 minutes' reading time. You can start sooner.
  2. Answer the examiner. Press Start speaking and talk, or type your answer. Press I've finished when you are done.
  3. Answer the follow-up questions. The examiner asks about points you haven't covered yet, up to 5 questions.
  4. Look at the topics to revise at the end.

Your browser will ask to use your microphone the first time you press Start speaking. Please don't mention real patients.
