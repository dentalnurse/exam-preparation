Coming soon

We are building an AI assessor you can talk to using your microphone, or by typing if you prefer. It is not available yet.

How it will work

  1. Choose a scenario and use the reading time to make notes.
  2. Talk to the assessor. Explain what you would do and why.
  3. Answer follow-up questions. The assessor will ask you to expand, the way a real assessor would.
  4. Reflect. Use the scenario checklist to see what you covered and what to revise.
This is a practice tool for your own preparation. AI is not always accurate, so if anything it says doesn't seem right, or you want to query something, ask your DNT assessor.

Until then

Use the practice scenarios with a timer and checklist, or ask a colleague to play the assessor.
