AI practice assessor

We are building an AI assessor that will run a Professional Discussion with you, just like the real thing. It is not available yet.

How it will work

  1. Choose a scenario and use the reading time to make notes.
  2. Talk to the assessor using your microphone, or type if you prefer.
  3. Answer follow-up questions. The assessor will ask about anything you missed, the way a real assessor would.
  4. Get a feedback report showing which points you covered for each AC and what to work on next.
The AI assessor will give practice feedback only. It will not decide whether you have met an AC. Your DNT assessor always makes that decision.

Until then

Use the practice scenarios with a timer and checklist, or ask a colleague to play the assessor.
